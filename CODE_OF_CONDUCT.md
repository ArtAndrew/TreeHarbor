# Community conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Critique designs and evidence, not people.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, disclosure of private information and spam
are not accepted. Respect consent and keep discussion relevant to the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation.
Enforcement should be proportionate and explained when doing so will not expose
private information. Raise concerns with the repository owner, ArtAndrew; do not
publish another person's private information in an issue.
